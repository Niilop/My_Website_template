This is an example post. Posts live in `src/content/blog/` as Markdown files. The front matter at
the top of this file sets the title, description, and dates.

## Formatting

You can use **bold**, _italic_, [links](https://docs.astro.build/), lists, and code:

```js
console.log('Hello!');
```

> Quotes work too.

To remove the blog entirely, follow the steps in the README.
